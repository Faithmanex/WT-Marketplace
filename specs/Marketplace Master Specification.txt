==================================================
ONE-PARAGRAPH DEVELOPER BRIEF
==================================================
This project implements a comprehensive Marketplace for Dr. William Triplett’s platform, showcasing books, digital media (audio, video, podcasts), print-on-demand apparel, courses, consulting services, speaking engagements, organizational development, faculty services, and a membership program. The site should display product descriptions and pricing while routing all transactions through external platforms. Development should follow a modular, reusable component-based approach suitable for Visual Studio Code, ensure mobile responsiveness, and deploy through Netlify.
